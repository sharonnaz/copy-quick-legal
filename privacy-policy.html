Privacy Policy for Copy Quick

Last updated: September 26, 2026 · Contact: sharon.naz@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Copy Quick (“the App”) handles information when you use it on iPhone or Android.

Summary

Copy Quick copies photos and videos from your device to storage you choose (for example an external drive or folder). The App does not operate a cloud backup service for your media and does not upload your photo or video library to our servers.

Information we access on your device

  • Photo Library / media files — to discover albums or folders and copy selected items to your destination.
  • Local storage statistics — to show approximate used and free space.
  • Files / folders you pick — via the system file picker so the App can write copies where you choose.
  • Purchase / entitlement status — via Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing to unlock and restore unlimited backups.

Information we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect, sell, or share the contents of your photos or videos, facial recognition data, advertising profiles, or analytics that track you across other apps. Media is read on-device for copying to your selected destination.

Purchases

If you buy the optional Unlock Unlimited purchase, payment is processed by Apple or Google. We only receive what the store provides to confirm entitlement. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Free usage tracking

The free plan may track how much data you have copied using storage on your device.

Permissions

You can revoke Photo Library or storage permissions in device Settings. Backup features that need those permissions will stop until access is granted again.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

The App may rely on Apple App Store / StoreKit, Google Play Billing, and system photo/file frameworks. Those services follow Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies.

Data retention

We do not host your media. Local App preferences and free-usage counters remain on your device until you delete the App or clear App data.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke media permissions in system Settings.
  • Delete the App to remove local App data.
  • Manage refunds through Apple or Google.
  • Email sharon.naz@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
