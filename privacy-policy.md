# Privacy Policy for Easy Backup

**Last updated:** September 22, 2026  
**App:** Easy Backup  
**Developer contact:** sharon.naz@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Easy Backup (“the App”) handles information when you use it on iPhone or Android.

## Summary

Easy Backup is designed to copy your photos and videos from your device to storage **you choose** (for example, an external drive or folder you select). The App does **not** operate a cloud backup service for your media, and it does **not** upload your photo or video library to our servers.

## Information we access on your device

Depending on the platform and the permissions you grant, the App may access:

- **Photo Library / media files** — to discover albums or folders and copy selected items to your chosen destination.
- **Local storage statistics** — to show approximate used and free space on your device.
- **Files / folders you pick** — through the system file picker, so the App can write copies to a destination you select.
- **Purchase / entitlement status** — through Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing, so the App can unlock unlimited backups after a one-time purchase and restore that purchase on the same store account.

## Information we do not collect

We do **not** intentionally collect, sell, or share:

- The contents of your photos or videos
- Facial recognition or photo analysis data
- Precise location from your media
- Advertising identifiers for third-party ads
- Analytics profiles that track you across other apps

Media is read on-device for the purpose of copying to your selected destination. We do not run a backend that stores your library.

## Purchases

If you buy the optional **Unlock Unlimited** in-app purchase:

- Payment is processed by **Apple** or **Google**, not by us.
- We receive only what the store provides to confirm entitlement (for example, that the product was purchased and is active).
- We do not receive your full payment card details.

## Free usage tracking

The free plan may track how much data you have copied (for example, toward a 1 GB free allowance) using storage **on your device**. That usage meter is local to the App unless the platform otherwise requires store-side records for purchases.

## Permissions

You can revoke Photo Library or storage-related permissions in your device Settings. If you revoke them, backup features that need those permissions will stop working until you grant access again.

## Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Third-party services

The App may rely on:

- Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS purchases and restore)
- Google Play Billing (Android purchases)
- System file and photo frameworks provided by Apple or Google

Those services are governed by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

## Data retention

Because we do not host your media, there is no media retention period on our servers. Local App preferences and free-usage counters remain on your device until you delete the App or clear App data.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke media permissions in system Settings.
- Delete the App to remove local App data on your device.
- Manage or request refunds for store purchases through Apple or Google according to their policies.
- Contact us at **sharon.naz@gmail.com** with privacy questions.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
